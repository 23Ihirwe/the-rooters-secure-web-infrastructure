TLS Certificate Information — Group The Rooters

Command used to generate:
sudo openssl req -x509 -nodes -days 365 -newkey rsa:2048 \
  -keyout /etc/ssl/private/the_rooters.key \
  -out /etc/ssl/certs/the_rooters.crt \
  -subj "/CN=localhost" \
  -addext "subjectAltName=DNS:localhost,IP:127.0.0.1"

Certificate inspection output:

subject=CN = localhost
issuer=CN = localhost
notBefore=Sep 12 16:43:49 2026 GMT
notAfter=Sep 12 16:43:49 2027 GMT

Field explanation:
- Subject: identity the certificate represents (localhost, our local test environment)
- Issuer: who signed the certificate. Matches Subject here, confirming it is self-signed.
- notBefore / notAfter: the certificate's validity window (365 days from generation)

File paths:
Certificate: /etc/ssl/certs/the_rooters.crt
Private key: /etc/ssl/private/the_rooters.key (NOT included in this submission)
